# 中文提示词

一张高度修图、光影完美的真人 Cosplay 创意摄影照片，画面清晰细腻，带有轻微胶片颗粒感和柔焦效果。

正面稍俯视角，中景构图。一位身材纤细、四肢修长的成年真人女性跪坐在柔软的床上。她一只手拿着平板电脑，平板电脑完美挡住自己的脸；平板屏幕亮起，屏幕中显示图像参考角色原画风格的脸部。她的另一只手在平板旁边伸出，比着剪刀手，姿态自然可爱。

重点强调"打破次元壁"的视觉错位效果：将平板视为一个恰好放在脸部位置的特殊发饰或面部窗口。女性的整个发型轮廓必须是完整、连续、不间断的，从头顶的猫耳、头饰，到两侧垂下的长发，形成一条完整自然的发型曲线。平板屏幕只是展示这条完整曲线中原本应该看到的脸部区域。因此，屏幕内的卡通头发必须与屏幕外的真人头发在颜色、走向、发量、体积、光影和边缘位置上绝对对齐，视觉上融为一体，不能断裂，不能错位，不能像单独贴上去的图片。

柔和的室内暖光从侧面照入，营造温暖、精致、干净的卧室摄影氛围。真人女性的发丝清晰可见，头发质感细腻自然。平板屏幕的冷色光微微照亮她的脖子、手指和衣服边缘。屏幕表面有极轻微反光，但不能影响屏幕内角色脸部的清晰度。整体画面像一张经过精细后期处理的高质量真人 Cosplay 摄影作品，真实摄影质感，细腻皮肤质感，清晰光影层次，创意感强，追求二次元角色与真人身体无缝融合的效果。

# English Prompt

A highly retouched, perfectly lit live-action cosplay creative photography portrait, clear and delicate image quality, with subtle film grain and a soft-focus effect.

Front-facing slightly high-angle view, medium shot composition. A slender adult woman with long, delicate limbs is kneeling on a soft bed. She is holding a tablet with one hand, and the tablet perfectly covers her face. The tablet screen is turned on and displays the face of the reference anime character in the original illustration style. Her other hand is stretched out beside the tablet, making a peace sign, with a natural and cute pose.

The key visual concept is a "breaking the dimensional wall" illusion. Treat the tablet as a special hair accessory or facial window placed exactly where the face should be. The woman's entire hairstyle silhouette must remain complete, continuous, and uninterrupted, from the cat ears on top of her head, to the hair accessories, to the long hair falling down on both sides. The tablet screen only reveals the facial area that would normally appear within this continuous hairstyle curve. Therefore, the cartoon hair inside the tablet screen must perfectly align with the real hair outside the screen in color, direction, volume, thickness, lighting, and edge position. It must visually merge into one seamless hairstyle, with no breaks, no misalignment, and no pasted-on feeling.

Soft warm indoor light enters from the side, creating a warm, refined, clean bedroom photography atmosphere. The real woman's hair strands are clearly visible, with natural and delicate hair texture. The cool light from the tablet screen gently illuminates her neck, fingers, and the edges of her clothing. The tablet screen has a very slight reflection, but it must not reduce the clarity of the anime face shown on the screen. The whole image should look like a carefully post-processed high-quality live-action cosplay photograph, with realistic photography texture, delicate skin texture, clear lighting layers, strong creative impact, and a seamless fusion between an anime character face and a real human body.
