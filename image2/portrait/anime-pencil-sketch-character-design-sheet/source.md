# 中文提示词
请仔细观察我上传的人物参考图片，先提取并锁定该角色的核心外貌特征，包括但不限于：性别、年龄气质、脸型与五官比例、发型、刘海形状、发长、发色与渐变、瞳色、眼型、眉形、服装款式、服装配色、重要配饰、标志性装饰物、整体角色气质与辨识度。

在重新绘制时，必须保持该角色的外貌特征绝对一致，不能改变角色身份，不能更换发型、发色、瞳色、服装结构或关键配饰。生成一张【二次元手稿风格的角色设定图】Character Design Sheet。

画风要求：精致的日系二次元风格，类似自动铅笔绘制的细腻素描线稿，pencil sketch，clean lineart，线条干净、轻盈、准确，带有画师草稿感但完成度很高。整体只使用一点点极其轻微的淡蓝色或柔和单色系水彩晕染上色，颜色非常克制，不能变成厚涂或完整彩稿。整体看起来像一本精美画集里的角色设定手稿页。

排版要求：画面像一本画集里的设定页，内容丰富且错落有致，构图有设计感，但背景保持干净。

画面中必须包含：

1. 一个全身站姿立绘作为主体，展示完整服装结构、发型轮廓、鞋袜或下装细节、身体比例和角色气质。
2. 周围散落 2 到 3 个半身或头部特写，用来展示不同表情，例如傲娇、困倦、微笑、惊讶、指着镜头、侧目、轻微不耐烦等，表情可根据原角色气质选择，但不能破坏角色设定。
3. 画面角落必须包含 1 到 2 个超可爱的 Q 版小人，Chibi style，例如抱着枕头、气呼呼鼓脸、挥手、坐在角落、抱着小物件等动作。
4. 可以加入少量服装局部细节放大图，例如发饰、领口、袖口、胸前装饰、腰部配件、鞋子、武器或随身小物件等，如果参考图中有明显道具或配饰，需要优先展示。

装饰元素：在画面的空白处随意点缀一些手写体的假名或英文小字，看起来像画师的随笔笔记；加入涂鸦小星星、爱心、回形针、涂鸦箭头、圆圈标注线、简短注释，或者一个小小的 barcode 条形码作为排版设计感。所有文字只作为装饰性视觉元素，不需要真实可读。

背景：干净纯白背景，或带有一点点细腻纸张纹理的白色背景。整体像扫描自画师速写本或设定集页面，干净、精致、轻盈。

最终效果：高质量角色设定图，日系二次元手稿风格，自动铅笔线稿，细腻素描，轻微淡蓝色/柔和单色水彩晕染，完整全身立绘，多表情头像，Q版小人，画集设定页排版，白色纸张背景，角色外貌与上传参考图保持绝对一致。

# English Prompt
Carefully observe the uploaded character reference image and first identify and lock the character's core visual traits, including but not limited to: gender, age impression, face shape and facial proportions, hairstyle, bang shape, hair length, hair color and gradients, eye color, eye shape, eyebrow shape, outfit style, outfit color scheme, important accessories, iconic decorations, overall character vibe, and recognizability.

When redrawing, keep the character's appearance absolutely consistent. Do not change the character identity, hairstyle, hair color, eye color, outfit structure, or key accessories. Create a Japanese anime-style Character Design Sheet.

Art style: refined Japanese anime style, delicate pencil sketch lineart like drawn with a mechanical pencil, clean lineart, precise and lightweight lines, with a subtle artist sketch feeling but highly polished. Use only a tiny amount of very pale blue or soft monochrome watercolor wash. The coloring must be extremely restrained, not full-color painting, not heavy rendering. The overall image should look like an elegant hand-drawn character design page from a premium art book.

Layout requirements: the image should look like a richly arranged character design sheet from an art book, with varied and balanced placement, visually designed composition, while keeping the background clean.

The image must include:

1. One full-body standing character illustration as the main subject, showing the complete outfit structure, hairstyle silhouette, shoes or lower-body details, body proportions, and character personality.
2. Around the main figure, scatter 2 to 3 bust or head close-ups showing different expressions, such as tsundere, sleepy, smiling, surprised, pointing at the viewer, sideways glance, or slightly annoyed. Choose expressions that fit the original character's vibe, without breaking the character design.
3. Include 1 to 2 ultra-cute Chibi versions of the character in the corners, such as hugging a pillow, puffing cheeks angrily, waving, sitting in the corner, or holding a small item.
4. Optionally include a few enlarged detail sketches of costume elements, such as hair accessories, collar, cuffs, chest ornament, waist accessories, shoes, weapon, or personal items. If the reference image contains iconic props or accessories, prioritize displaying them.

Decorative elements: place some handwritten kana or small English notes in the blank areas, looking like an artist's casual annotations; add doodle stars, hearts, paper clips, doodle arrows, circled callouts, short notes, or a tiny barcode as graphic design elements. All text is decorative and does not need to be readable.

Background: clean pure white background, or white background with a subtle fine paper texture. The whole image should feel like a scanned page from an artist's sketchbook or official design book, clean, refined, and lightweight.

Final result: high-quality character design sheet, Japanese anime hand-drawn sketch style, mechanical pencil lineart, delicate pencil drawing, subtle pale blue or soft monochrome watercolor wash, full-body standing figure, multiple expression portraits, cute chibi figures, art-book design sheet layout, white paper background, with the character's appearance absolutely consistent with the uploaded reference image.
