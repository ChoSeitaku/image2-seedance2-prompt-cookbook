# 中文提示词

绘制一张以图一角色为主要参考对象的脸部特写画作。画面由多种不同时代的电子设备屏幕共同拼合而成，像一幅由屏幕组成的碎片化肖像拼贴。角色形象以图一为基础，保留她最具代表性的面部特征、发型、气质与灰色眼睛。她端坐着，微笑着看向镜头，灰色眼睛闪烁着明亮光芒，整体氛围神秘、安静、带有轻微科技感与注视感。

画面主体是一张完整的人物脸部特写，但这张脸不是直接绘制在单一画布上，而是被拆分显示在多台电子设备的屏幕里。设备包括老年机、翻盖手机、早期智能手机、现代智能手机、MP4 播放器、BP机、掌上游戏机、小型数码播放器等。不同设备的屏幕共同拼合成角色完整的脸部、眼睛、鼻子、嘴巴、头发和肩颈区域。每块屏幕只显示人物肖像的一部分，多个屏幕组合后形成完整图片。

部分屏幕上出现多种语言的文字"我正在看着你"，例如中文、英文、日文、韩文、法文、西班牙文、德文等；也可以由多个屏幕组合成"我正在看着你"的多语言文字。文字应自然显示在屏幕内容中，像系统弹窗、短信、字幕、通知或屏幕叠加文字，不要覆盖全部人物脸部，不要破坏主体识别。

不同设备因为年代、屏幕材质和显示能力不同，画质存在差异：老年机和 BP 机屏幕分辨率较低，可能出现像素感、噪点、色偏、扫描线、低饱和度或显示残缺；翻盖手机和 MP4 屏幕略微模糊；现代智能手机屏幕更清晰、色彩更准确。整体拼贴画面允许因设备不规则摆放、屏幕边框、屏幕比例限制而产生轻微遮挡、不显示、错位、分割和画面断裂，但角色脸部整体仍能被识别为一张完整特写。

构图要求：所有电子设备以真实物理尺寸和真实屏幕比例摆放，不得出现不规则形状的电子屏幕，不得出现不符合现实的弯曲屏幕、漂浮屏幕、无边框异形屏幕或任意裁切的屏幕碎片。手机屏幕必须符合实际尺寸和实际长宽比例。设备可以轻微倾斜、叠放、错落摆放，但屏幕形状必须是现实存在的矩形或圆角矩形。画面最外围不要铺满电子设备，需要保留适当留白，让整体构图更高级、更像艺术摄影或装置艺术作品。

整体风格为高质量概念艺术与精修摄影结合，细节丰富，屏幕玻璃有轻微反光，设备外壳具有真实材质，包括塑料、金属、磨损边角、旧按键、指纹和细小划痕。光线柔和，画面清晰，带有轻微胶片颗粒感和科技怀旧感。最终画面应像一件由电子设备拼装而成的角色肖像装置艺术摄影作品。

# English Prompt

Create a close-up portrait artwork based primarily on the character in Image 1. The image is assembled from the screens of electronic devices from different eras, forming a fragmented portrait mosaic made of displays. The character should be based on the reference image, preserving her most recognizable facial features, hairstyle, aura, and signature gray eyes. She is sitting calmly, smiling while looking directly at the camera. Her gray eyes glow with a bright sparkle, creating a mysterious, quiet atmosphere with a subtle technological and watchful feeling.

The main subject is a complete close-up portrait of the character's face, but the face is not drawn on a single canvas. Instead, it is split across many electronic device screens. The devices include senior phones, flip phones, early smartphones, modern smartphones, MP4 players, pagers, handheld game consoles, small digital media players, and other retro electronic devices. The screens of these devices collectively form the character's full face, eyes, nose, mouth, hair, neck, and shoulders. Each screen shows only one part of the portrait, and together the screens assemble into one complete image.

Some screens display the phrase "I am watching you" in multiple languages, such as Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, French, Spanish, German, and others. Alternatively, multiple screens may combine to form multilingual versions of "I am watching you." The text should appear naturally within the screen content, like system pop-ups, messages, subtitles, notifications, or screen overlay text. It should not cover the entire face or destroy the recognizability of the portrait.

Because the devices come from different eras and have different display materials and capabilities, the image quality varies across screens. Senior phones and pagers may show low resolution, pixelation, noise, color shifts, scan lines, low saturation, or incomplete display areas. Flip phones and MP4 player screens may appear slightly blurry. Modern smartphone screens should be clearer with more accurate colors. The overall mosaic may include slight occlusion, missing display areas, misalignment, segmentation, and visual breaks caused by irregular device placement, bezels, and screen aspect ratio limitations. However, the character's face should still be recognizable as one complete close-up portrait.

Composition requirements: all electronic devices must be arranged with realistic physical sizes and realistic screen proportions. Do not create irregularly shaped electronic screens. Do not use unrealistic curved screens, floating screens, borderless fantasy screens, or randomly cut screen fragments. Phone screens must follow real-world dimensions and real-world aspect ratios. The devices may be slightly tilted, stacked, overlapped, or arranged unevenly, but every screen must remain a realistic rectangle or rounded rectangle. The outermost area of the image should not be filled with electronic devices; leave appropriate negative space around the composition to make it feel more refined, like art photography or an installation artwork.

The overall style should combine high-quality concept art with polished photography. Rich details, subtle glass reflections on screens, realistic device materials such as plastic and metal, worn edges, old buttons, fingerprints, and tiny scratches. Soft lighting, clear image quality, subtle film grain, and a nostalgic technological mood. The final image should look like a character portrait installation artwork assembled from electronic devices.
